Why We Started This

Junk mail went straight into the recycling, and our stickers were being ignored. We needed clear stickers that junk mailers understand and respect. Plus, less junk mail means less waste! 🌱

Our Family Story 🇨🇭

Started in 2025 when our kids got frustrated with our overflowing mailbox. They sketched the first design, we figured out the production, and now we help Swiss families take back control of their mail.

We're passionate about reducing waste too. Every piece of junk mail prevented is paper saved, trees protected, and less recycling burden on our beautiful Swiss environment. 🌲

We're a small family business based in Switzerland. Every sticker is made with care, and we know each customer matters. Quality over quantity, always.
